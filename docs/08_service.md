# 8. Service

> Generated from the CAD and BOM data (counts are taken from the model, not typed). Numbers marked [estimate] are modelled or unverified; everything else is counted from the model or read from a seller's page.

Layers come off in this order: **body → deck → layshaft → bulkhead → wheel → upright**. Times are estimates (1 min plus
0.6 min a screw and 0.2 min a snap).

| Wear item | Layers off | Screws | Snaps | Min [estimate] | Tools | Last step |

- **body:** body off: 2 twist keys a quarter turn, slide 9.5 mm (R22)
- **deck:** deck off: 11 deck screws (bulkheads, motor plate, lay holder; 4 of them through the tower feet) + 4 shock top-eye screws, pop the 2 tie-rod cups and the servo link
- **layshaft:** layshaft out: M4 nyloc off the lay holder, slide the 5 mm shoulder axle out; the drum drops free
- **bulkhead:** one bulkhead piece off: 4 floor screws from below, that side's lower-arm hinge bolt (nyloc) and upper-link pin; slide that side's CVD out of the outdrive
- **wheel:** wheel off: M4 flange nyloc
- **upright:** upright / knuckle off: 2 pins (rear) or 2 ball-stud cups (front)

## Intervals [estimate]

- After every run: look at both belts and the pinion; wipe the tyres.
- Every 5 packs: re-snug the 2 motor screws; check the CVD pins and the wheel nyloc.
- After any crash: battery out first; check the bumper, front mount and arms; reprint the bumper if it cracked.
