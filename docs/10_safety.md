# 10. LiPo and high-speed safety

> Generated from the CAD and BOM data (counts are taken from the model, not typed). Numbers marked [estimate] are modelled or unverified; everything else is counted from the model or read from a seller's page.

## LiPo

- Charge out of the car, on a balance charger, on a non-flammable surface, never unattended.
- A pack that is puffed, dented, hot after a crash or smells sweet is finished: take it outside, let it sit somewhere it
  can burn safely, then take it to a battery recycler.
- After a hard crash: unplug the pack first, then look at it.
- Store at storage charge (about 3.8 V a cell).
- The LiPo bag lining in the bay is a brief flame shield, not containment.

## High speed

- 81.9 mph is about 132 km/h: a 2 kg car at that speed can break bones. Run only on closed, empty
  ground with nobody downrange, never on a public road.
- Runway: the 80 mph tier needs about 106 m to reach 90 % of top speed, plus room to stop. Mark a run-off area.
- Transmitter on first, off last. Test fail-safe before every session.
- Never hold the car with the wheels on the ground and the pack connected.
- Eye protection when testing at speed.
