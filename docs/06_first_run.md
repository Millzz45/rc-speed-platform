# 6. First-run setup

> Generated from the CAD and BOM data (counts are taken from the model, not typed). Numbers marked [estimate] are modelled or unverified; everything else is counted from the model or read from a seller's page.

Run these in order. Do not go faster until the previous line passes.

| # | Test | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Weigh the car ready to run (kitchen scale) | 80 mph: about 2046<!--D:mass_g=2046--> g [modelled]; 50 mph about 1871 g |
| 2 | Wheels up, 25 % throttle for 2 min, touch the motor plate | warm, not hot; no belt skip |
| 3 | Leaf blower at the nose, car on 2 kitchen scales | each axle loses ≤ 25 % of its weight |
| 4 | Runway at a 50 % throttle end-point, phone GPS app | straight, no wobble |
| 5 | 5 passes with a 50 °C sticker on the motor plate | sticker has not turned |
| 6 | 100 % end-point, step up 10 mph a pass | 80 mph tier: about 81.5 mph modelled; 50 mph tier about 55.6 mph |
| 7 | 10 full-throttle launches, then look at both belts | no skipped or torn teeth |

**Radio setup before test 4:** fail-safe to neutral; throttle end-point 50 %; steering end-points so the servo never
pushes against the lock; ESC punch at its lowest setting; ESC LiPo cut-off for your cell count.
